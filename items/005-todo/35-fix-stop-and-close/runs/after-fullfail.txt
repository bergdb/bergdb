fullfail. A database with a 100 KB buffer on a stream whose appends fail after 50 KB: a put of 1 MB, which waits for room in the buffer; then a put of 10 bytes in another thread.

  the put of 10 bytes threw bergdb.pub.util.DbFailedException: Cannot call execute(): the database failed (the cause) and must be restarted.
